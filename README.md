# 🍟 McDonald's Business Performance Dashboard (Excel)

## 🧾 Project Overview
This project presents an **interactive Excel Dashboard** that analyzes **McDonald’s business performance** across multiple metrics, including **sales, profit, regional trends, and key performance indicators (KPIs)**.

The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders and analysts **understand McDonald’s operational efficiency, revenue patterns, and growth opportunities** through clear visual storytelling.

---

## 🎯 Objective
- To visualize and track McDonald’s overall performance metrics.
- To analyze sales, revenue, and profit across different **regions, products, and time periods**.
- To identify **key factors driving profit and growth**.
- To create an easy-to-use and interactive **Excel dashboard** for strategic decision-making.

---

## 🧮 Dataset Description
The dataset used for this project represents McDonald’s sales and performance data across various outlets and time periods.

| **Column Name** | **Description** |
|:--|:--|
| `Order ID` | Unique identifier for each order |
| `Date` | Transaction date |
| `Region` | Location or operational region |
| `Product Category` | Type of product sold (e.g., Burgers, Beverages, Fries) |
| `Quantity` | Units sold per order |
| `Sales` | Revenue generated from the order |
| `Profit` | Profit earned from the order |
| `Cost` | Operational or production cost |
| `Customer Type` | Category of customer (e.g., Regular, New) |

**Data Source:** Simulated business data representing McDonald’s retail performance  
**Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel  

---

## ⚙️ Methodology
1. **Data Preparation**
   - Cleaned and formatted raw sales data.
   - Removed inconsistencies and duplicates.
   - Created calculated fields for total sales, profit margins, and growth trends.

2. **Data Analysis**
   - Used **PivotTables** and **PivotCharts** for trend analysis.
   - Created key performance measures: total sales, total profit, and average order value.
   - Compared performance across **regions, categories, and time**.

3. **Dashboard Design**
   - Built KPI cards for quick insights.
   - Integrated filters and slicers for interactive exploration.
   - Used Excel formulas and conditional formatting to highlight trends.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Components

| **Section** | **Visual Type** | **Description** | **Key Insight** |
|--------------|----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **KPI Summary** | Card Visuals | Shows total sales, total profit, number of orders, and average profit margin | Provides an at-a-glance summary of business performance |
| **Sales by Region** | Clustered Column Chart | Compares total sales across McDonald’s regional outlets | Identifies the best and least performing regions |
| **Product Category Performance** | Pie Chart | Displays revenue contribution by category (e.g., Burgers, Drinks, Desserts) | Highlights which menu items drive most revenue |
| **Monthly Sales Trend** | Line Chart | Shows sales and profit over time | Detects seasonality and monthly sales growth |
| **Profit vs Cost Analysis** | Combo Chart | Compares total profit and total cost | Reveals operational efficiency and profit margins |
| **Top 10 Products by Sales** | Horizontal Bar Chart | Lists highest-grossing items | Identifies customer favorites and high-margin products |
| **Customer Segment Analysis** | Donut Chart | Visualizes revenue share by customer type | Shows which customer group contributes most to sales |

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Burgers and Beverages** contribute the highest share of revenue.
- **Region-wise analysis** shows that certain outlets consistently outperform others.
- **Profit margins** remain stable but vary with seasonal promotions.
- **Repeat customers** generate a significant portion of overall sales.
- Cost optimization in select regions can further increase net profit.
